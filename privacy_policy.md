# Folienta Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Folienta is a local-only reader for DRM-free EPUB and PDF files that you choose to import.

## Data stored on your device

Folienta stores imported book files, cover images, book metadata, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reader preferences, and reading activity inside the app’s private local container. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

## Advertising and data collection

Folienta does not require an account. Folienta uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent and privacy choices where required. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, and performance data for third-party advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google’s handling of this information is governed by Google’s policies.

Folienta disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier. The app does not send your imported publications, covers, metadata, reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reader settings, or reading activity to Google or to the developer.

Where required, the app requests consent before requesting ads. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for your region, you can reopen it from Folienta Settings.

## Network access and external links

Folienta connects to Google services for advertising and consent management. Folienta does not operate a book store, catalog, cloud sync service, or content delivery service. An imported publication may contain a hyperlink to an external website. Opening an external link is your choice and is then governed by the destination’s privacy practices.

## Device backups

Local app data may be included in device backups according to your iOS and backup-provider settings. Folienta does not operate or control those backup services.

## Your choices

You can delete an individual book and its related data from Library, or delete all Folienta data from Settings. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to your device backup settings.

## Content rights

You are responsible for importing only DRM-free files that you legally own or are authorized to use. Folienta does not remove, bypass, or defeat digital rights management.

## Children

Folienta is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through accounts or book features. Google Mobile Ads processing is subject to Google’s policies and the consent controls described above.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and published policy will be updated before the changed app version is released.
