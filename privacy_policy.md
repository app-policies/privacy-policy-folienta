# Folienta Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Folienta is a local-only reader for DRM-free EPUB and PDF files that you choose to import.

## Data stored on your device

Folienta stores imported book files, cover images, book metadata, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reader preferences, and reading activity inside the app’s private local container. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

## Data collection and sharing

Folienta does not require an account. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data through the app. Folienta contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Network access and external links

Folienta does not operate a book store, catalog, cloud sync service, or content delivery service. An imported publication may contain a hyperlink to an external website. Opening an external link is your choice and is then governed by the destination’s privacy practices.

## Device backups

Local app data may be included in device backups according to your iOS and backup-provider settings. Folienta does not operate or control those backup services.

## Your choices

You can delete an individual book and its related data from Library, or delete all Folienta data from Settings. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to your device backup settings.

## Content rights

You are responsible for importing only DRM-free files that you legally own or are authorized to use. Folienta does not remove, bypass, or defeat digital rights management.

## Children

Folienta does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and published policy will be updated before the changed app version is released.

## Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to support@passmate.jp.
